Force Hacks

Some websites have a chance of triggering a hack when you visit them. You can utilize this in order to force hacks and gain rep.

These sites are marked in the wiki block with a different color, and the chance for them to hack you is displayed next to the name.

Note that after a successful hack attempt this behavior goes on cooldown. The cooldown time varies on the chance:
  • 87% Chance - 4 minutes 15 seconds
  • 40% Chance - 3 minutes
